# Data: sources, licences and snapshot dates

Everything the project needs ships in this folder. Nothing is fetched at run time.

## Flood event: Assam floods 2022

- **Source:** Government of Assam, *Memorandum on Assam Floods 2022* to the Ministry of Home Affairs (prepared with ASDMA), tables 2.5 (population and villages affected), 2.6 (lives lost) and 2.7 (relief camps and inmates), pages 17–21 of the PDF.
  <https://igr.assam.gov.in/sites/default/files/swf_utility_folder/departments/asdma_revenue_uneecopscloud_com_oid_70/menu/document/assam_flood_memorandum_2022.pdf>
- **Period covered:** 6 April – 28 October 2022, in three waves; the June wave was the peak. The state total is 8,850,686 people affected in 35 districts.
- **What `population` means:** people affected per district, **cumulative over the whole period**. Someone affected in two waves may be counted twice. It is not a single-day snapshot.
- **Districts used:** the 25 districts with the largest affected population. The 10 left out each had fewer than 40,000 people affected.
- **Licence:** a public government document with no open licence stated, used here with attribution.
- **Verification:** we checked every population, village, death and relief-camp number in `targets.csv` against the PDF tables.

### `targets.csv` columns

| column | meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `target_id`, `name` | district ID and name |
| `lat`, `lon` | district headquarters town; the town is named in `source` |
| `population` | people affected, from memorandum table 2.5 |
| `priority` | 1–5, derived by the rule below |
| `villages`, `deaths`, `camp_inmates` | memorandum tables 2.5, 2.6 and 2.7 (`deaths` is blank where a district is not in table 2.6) |
| `displacement_share` | `camp_inmates / population` |

**Priority rule (our own; not in the source):** each district gets the quintile (1–5) of its displacement share among the 25 districts, plus 1 if it recorded 10 or more deaths, capped at 5. Displacement share stands in for how badly people were forced out of their homes. The rule and every typed-in number live in `preprocessing/build_targets.py`.

**Coordinates:** taken from the Wikipedia article of the headquarters town (CC BY-SA 4.0) or from Wikidata property P625 (CC0); `source` says which. Amingaon and Mushalpur are only accurate to about 0.1°.

## Orbits: `tles.txt`

- CelesTrak GP data in TLE format, downloaded **2026-10-10 12:52 UTC**. Element epochs are day 281–283 of 2026, i.e. 8–10 Oct 2026. <https://celestrak.org/NORAD/elements/gp.php>
- **Satellites, all present in CelesTrak:**

  | satellite | NORAD ID |
  | --- | --- |
  | Sentinel-1A | 39634 |
  | Sentinel-1C | 62261 |
  | Sentinel-1D | 66315 |
  | NISAR | 65053 |
  | EOS-4 (RISAT-1A) | 51656 |
  | Capella-13 | 60419 |
  | Capella-14 | 59444 |
  | Capella-15 | 60544 |

- **Left out:** Sentinel-1B, which is retired.
- **Licence:** CelesTrak data is freely available. Credit: CelesTrak / Dr T.S. Kelso.
- **Important:** CelesTrak only serves current elements. The pass geometry is therefore the **2026 constellation replayed over the 2022 flood locations**, for a 48 h window starting 2026-10-11 00:00 UTC. It is not the set of passes that existed in June 2022.

## `windows.json`

Generated by `preprocessing/compute_windows.py` (documented in its header). A window is kept when the incidence angle at broadside (closest approach, i.e. zero Doppler) lies within 20–45°. Each window records `satellite`, `target_id`, `t_start` and `t_end` (the part of the pass inside the incidence range), `t_broadside`, `best_incidence_deg`, `look_side` and `pass_id`.

## Region outline: `region.geojson`, `meta.json`

- Assam boundary from Natural Earth 1:50m admin-1 states and provinces, public domain. <https://www.naturalearthdata.com/>
- Natural Earth draws de facto boundaries, which may differ from the official Survey of India map. We use the outline for display only.

## Generated files

- `instance_default.json` and `params.json` are written by `preprocessing/run_experiments.py`. They hold the small default instance that the Run button uses: its clusters, warm-start angles and tuned QAOA parameters.
